# Is it worth making into a skill?

Use this before building anything the person brought themselves, and when choosing between several things found in their work. Say the result in one line. Never show this table.

| Check | Ask yourself | It fits when | If it does not |
|---|---|---|---|
| More than one question or one document | Is it a whole piece of work built from several sources? | A report, pack, deck, workbook or response assembled from several files or systems | A single question, a summary of one document, meeting notes or inbox triage: say Copilot Chat already does this well |
| More than rules | Does it need reading, assembling and drafting? | Each one differs enough to need judgement in the drafting | High-volume matching, routing or approvals inside set rules: say this belongs to automation, and offer the exceptions that need a person |
| Checkable | Can each point be traced to a source? | Every figure and statement can cite a file, a row or a clause | A forecast, valuation, diagnosis or attribution nobody can check: offer the evidence pack and leave the judgement with the person |
| A person decides | Who signs it off? | A named person reviews and decides, and Cowork drafts | The output would itself be the decision (price, approve, pay, hire): offer the draft for that person |
| It comes round again | How often, and for how many people? | Weekly, monthly or quarterly, or many similar ones a year | A one-off: do it once in the conversation, and do not build a skill |
| Something moves | What changes when it is right first time? | A deadline, a queue, a send-back rate or outside spend that someone owns | Nothing an owner would notice: say so, and suggest one that has an owner |
| Reachable and fit to use | Can the inputs be read now? | Files in Microsoft 365 or exports, and past, finished examples | Complaint, claim, patient or staff records: start with past cases with personal details removed |

## Reshape rather than refuse

- **Volume to exceptions.** Leave matching and routing with automation. Take the exceptions that need several sources assembled.
- **Judgement to evidence.** Leave the decision with the person. Produce the evidence pack and the options.
- **One document to the pack.** Widen one article to the whole pack, or one answer to the whole response.
- **Sensitive records to past cases.** Use past, decided cases with personal details removed.

## How to say it

- It fits: "This suits Cowork: it is a monthly pack from several sources, your controller reviews it, and every line can cite its source."
- It does not: "This one is quicker in Copilot Chat, because it is a single document. The part that would suit Cowork is the whole monthly pack."

## What it is worth

Name the measure that moves first. Money counts only where real spend can stop (contractors, overtime, outside advisers) or where revenue depends on the work (bids answered, renewals kept). Never turn time into money. Use launchpad-business-case for the sums.
