# Launchpad for Cowork 2.2.0: install and use

Launchpad for Cowork finds work that Cowork can take off your plate, shows what it would make,
builds it on your own files, tests it on a past period and leaves you with skills you keep and
routines that run. It is one plugin with five skills and one connector.

## Install it for yourself
1. In Cowork, open **Customize**, then the **Plugins** tab.
2. Select **Upload plugin** and choose `launchpad-for-cowork-2.2.0.zip`.
3. In the **Share** dialog, choose who can use it: **Only you**, or **Specific users in your
   organization**. Select **Apply**.
4. Start a new conversation and type **Get me started**.

## Install it for your organisation (admins)
1. In the **Microsoft 365 admin center**, open **Manage apps**, then **Upload custom app**.
2. Select the ellipsis, then **Add agent**, and upload `launchpad-for-cowork-2.2.0.zip`.
3. Choose who can use it.
4. People find it under **Plugins** in Cowork, then start a new conversation and type
   **Get me started**.

## What it reads
- Nothing until it has welcomed you and you say **Have a look**.
- Then only: the titles of your meetings and how many people attend (two weeks back and two
  weeks ahead), the subjects of mail you sent in the last two weeks, and the names of files you
  edited in the last 30 days, including spreadsheets several people edit.
- Never message bodies, attachments or file contents for that look. It leaves out anything
  private, confidential, personal, HR or medical.
- Reading a whole file, such as an example of your monthly pack, always needs your OK for that
  one file.
- You can try it on invented sample data, or answer three quick questions instead, and nothing of
  yours is read.

## What it keeps
- Nothing without your OK.
- Your profile is saved to your Cowork folder (Launchpad/profile.md) only if you choose
  **Save my profile**. Say "Show my Launchpad profile", "Change my profile" or "Forget my profile"
  at any time. The profile does not give permission for future reads.
- Skills it builds for you are saved to the skills folder in your Cowork folder only when you
  choose **Keep it**, using Cowork's own skill saving.
- It never sends, posts, shares or schedules anything without your approval of that exact action.
  Questions to colleagues are drafted, never sent.

## The five skills
| Skill | Say | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| launchpad-start | "Get me started", "What do people in my industry do with Cowork?" | The welcome, a look at your recent work with your OK, "Is this your work?", what people like you do, and your profile |
| launchpad-cookbook | "Show me the cookbook", "Co-develop this with me", "Turn my monthly pack into a skill" | Recipes and the wider cookbook (work that people in your role and industry hand to Cowork, with a short film where there is one), an example, learning from one of your files, build, test on a past period, keep |
| launchpad-business-case | "Is it worth it?" | The measure that moves, cash that can really stop, and Cowork's running cost, by script |
| launchpad-routines | "Help me stay on top of my day" | Monday priorities, a morning brief, meeting preparation, skills on a schedule or an email |
| launchpad-how-do-i | "How do I share a skill?" | Answers about using Cowork, from Microsoft Learn |

## The cookbook
The wider cookbook covers financial services in depth (banking, capital markets, asset and wealth
management, insurance, payments) and every other major industry by role and kind of work. It
holds patterns of recurring work, never anyone's data: Launchpad uses your role, your industry and
your country to pick what to show, and nothing about other organisations is ever shown or counted.

## The Microsoft Learn connector
The plugin includes one connector, **Microsoft Learn**. It searches Microsoft's official public
documentation to answer "How do I...?" questions about Cowork. It is read-only and needs no
sign-in, and it sees only the question being searched.

## Pictures
Cards show pictures from scott-adams.ai, the site of the Cowork Cookbook. Every card makes sense
without them: if a picture does not load, the text says the same thing.

## Good to know
- Custom plugins and their skills are not available in Cowork on mobile. Use Cowork in the
  browser or the desktop app.
- Cowork keeps one schedule per conversation, up to 25 in all. Launchpad sets up one routine at a
  time and gives you a one-line starter for the others. Manage them on the **Automations** page.
- Figures in cards and in the skills it builds always come from scripts, never typed by hand.
  Money appears only where real spend can stop, and time is never turned into money.

## Remove it
Open **Customize**, then **Plugins**, select Launchpad for Cowork and remove it. Skills you kept
stay in your Cowork folder until you delete them from **Customize**, **Skills**. Your profile
stays until you say "Forget my profile".
