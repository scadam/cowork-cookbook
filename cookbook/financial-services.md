# Cowork Cookbook: financial services

Work that people in financial services hand to Copilot Cowork. Launchpad for Cowork uses this menu to suggest work, show a short film, and co-develop the skill on the person's own files.

Every entry passes the same checks: it is a whole piece of work from several sources, every point can be traced to a source, a named person decides, it comes round again, and something an owner cares about moves.
